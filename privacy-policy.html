Nova Rescue Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nova Rescue ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

The App stores gameplay progress locally on your device, such as best score, unlocked chapters, selected pet, talents, coins, and settings. This information is used only to run the game and improve your gameplay experience.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or contacts.

Advertising

The App may show rewarded ads or interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are optional and may be used to receive in-game benefits such as revives, bonus rewards, or extra help during a run.

The App may use Google AdMob or similar advertising services. These providers may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app usage data, diagnostics, and similar information to deliver, measure, and improve ads. Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

In-App Purchases

The App may offer purchases such as removing ads or buying optional items. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers only as needed to provide app functionality, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Data Retention

Gameplay progress stored locally remains on your device until you delete the App or clear the relevant app data. Data handled by third-party service providers is retained according to their own policies.

Your Choices

You can reset local gameplay progress by deleting the App from your device. You can limit ad tracking through your iOS privacy settings. If the App offers a remove-ads purchase, you can use it to remove forced advertising placements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
